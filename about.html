Our story

A studio built around feeling good.

Lumière began with a simple idea: beauty appointments should feel less like a transaction and more like time set aside for yourself.

Today, our small team brings together thoughtful technique, warm hospitality and a little bit of everyday magic. Whether you’re here for a trim, a colour transformation or the morning before your wedding, we want you to leave feeling looked after.

  • Eight years of creative beauty work
  • Continued education and considered products
  • A calm studio where every detail has a purpose
